Privacy Policy

JoSecure is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users, clients, and workforce data. This policy explains how we collect, process, store, and protect information when you use our platform.

Data we collect

We may collect account details, company information, attendance records, job or shift metadata, location and device data, usage analytics, and contact information required to provide the platform.

How we use data

We use personal and operational data to deliver attendance monitoring, detect anomalies, produce compliance reports, and improve the accuracy and reliability of our AI workflows. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and security

Data is stored in secure cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. We apply access controls, logging, monitoring, and retention policies to reduce risk and maintain platform integrity.

Third parties

We may use trusted cloud providers and operational tooling to support hosting, monitoring, and delivery. These providers are selected to support security and privacy requirements.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, correction of inaccurate records, or deletion of personal information where the law allows. Contact us at hello@josecure.com to exercise those rights.

Updates

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the platform after changes indicates acceptance of the updated terms.
